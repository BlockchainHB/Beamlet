A small home for your connection

See whether Remote Control is starting, online, reconnecting or needs attention. When connected, open Claude or use the connection link to continue on another device.

Choose your starting folder and optional startup, power and notification preferences. After initial setup, Beamlet manages the connection without a terminal window dedicated to it.

What you need

The current version requires a compatible Claude Code installation, an account eligible for Remote Control, and completed sign-in, workspace trust and consent. Your Mac must stay powered on and connected to the internet.
